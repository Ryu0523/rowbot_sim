# Design: a planing plant that is physically credible at any speed (version "as1")

Scope: the scarab195 hull. The current plant is `sim/planing_vessel.py` (pv) with the large-roll subclass `sim/planing_vessel_gz.py` (gz) and the waterjet `sim/waterjet.py` (wj). The target covers rest, displacement, the hump, transition and planing (35+ kn), in waves from every heading. Nothing tracked was modified.

This design pass added one analysis script, `studies/anyspeed_design_check.py`. Its outputs are `studies/_cache/anyspeed/design_check.txt` (parts 1-3) and `design_check_sunk.txt` (part 4).

## 0. Summary and key decisions

- **One gap behind most symptoms: two terms the plant was missing.**
  - The plant applies the infinite-Froude, dry-transom planing flow (Zarnick-type 2D+t with a zero-pressure free surface, "phi=0") at every speed.
  - It lacks the transom-face pressure and the full time derivative of the sectional momentum.
  - Restoring those two terms is exact physics, with no new empirical input. It removes:
    - the 439 N sternward force at rest;
    - the doubled longitudinal wave force;
    - the 1-9 kn self-oscillation;
    - the coexisting sunk branch (the "fold") and the trim jump between 9 and 10 kn.
- **Organising principle (general, no regime blends of the force law):**
  - (i) Use the nonlinear 2D+t as the infinite-Froude limit, with a complete D/Dt.
  - (ii) Compute hydrostatics and the incident-wave (Froude-Krylov) force exactly, by integrating pressure over the instantaneous wetted surface, transom face included.
  - (iii) Add every missing gravity effect as a linear correction defined as [theory with gravity] minus [the same theory in its phi=0 limit], on the same geometry. Such a correction vanishes as the Froude number goes to infinity, so it leaves the validated planing behaviour alone and cannot double count.
  - (iv) Make the 3D (non-slender) correction needed at low speed follow the transom flow state.
  - (v) Model one physical regime variable: the wetted fraction of the transom, eps.
- **Empirical inputs are kept only where the physics is viscous or separated:**
  - transom ventilation (Doctors et al. 2007; Maki et al. 2005; Saunders 1957);
  - Zarnick's dry-transom buoyancy factor of 0.5;
  - friction;
  - cross-flow drag;
  - chine eddy roll damping;
  - the jet cavitation limit.
- **Two bridging assumptions in the 8-18 kn band are NOT derived.** They are labelled and tested for whether they carry the result (step S8):
  - a_b changes linearly with eps;
  - the 3D low-speed radiation terms are weighted by the mean-state eps.
- **Planing (eps = 0, about 18 kn and above):** the model reduces to v0 except where v0 is shown wrong:
  - the doubled wave force Xw is removed;
  - roll at speed now has physical stiffness;
  - lateral masses follow the instantaneous immersion (they equal v0's frozen values at the 25 kn equilibrium);
  - wave orbital velocities enter the relative flow.

**What I checked myself where the four reports disagree or were incomplete:**

1. **Hydrostatic identity at rest** (design_check part 1):
   - bottom term -439.4 N, taper -35.0 N, wetted transom face +531.3 N; residual +57 N (0.4 % of weight).
   - The residual is the unclosed forward face of the last strip, about 73 N. The yc ~ sqrt(1-s^2) taper is singular at the stem.
   - So the audit's "-92 N taper" is really taper plus stem closure.
   - Implementation consequence: write the bottom force in the exact telescoped form, -rho g Q_T - rho g ∫A eta_x dx, and never differentiate the geometry.
2. **Which longitudinal wave force is the copy.** By the divergence theorem applied to each slice, the plant's bottom term -rho g Σ A tau dx IS the true bottom hydrostatic plus wave-pressure force at every speed.
   - The separate slope force Xw (pv:300,302) is the duplicate at every speed.
   - What depends on the transom state is the face force, not which term to drop.
3. **The fold is caused by the missing D/Dt term (R3), not by a_b alone.** Rebuilt captive equations plus the missing -u V dm_a/dx|_d term (part 2):
   - The displacement branch is stable from 1 kn upward.
   - Trim rises continuously (1.90, 2.23, 2.98, 3.73, 4.51, 5.45 deg at 1, 5, 7, 8, 9, 10 kn) and joins the planing branch with no jump.
   - Newton from the audit's sunk equilibria at 10-25 kn no longer finds the low-trim sunk branch (part 4).
   - Equilibria at 14-25 kn are identical to v0 to 4 decimals, so the planing range is untouched.
4. **a_b carries the transition result** (part 3, with the complete D/Dt):
   - At 10 kn, trim is 3.46 / 4.82 / 7.21 deg for a_b = 1 / 0.75 / 0.5; at 12 kn, 3.88 / 5.16 deg for a_b = 1 / 0.75.
   - At 16 kn 3.75 vs 4.76 deg, and at 20 kn 2.88 vs 3.51 deg (a_b = 1 vs 0.5).
   - The fold-free branch does not make the a_b closure harmless; it is the main empirical risk.
5. **The negative damping in the audit's section C at 10-35 kn belongs to the sunk branch.** The planing branch is well damped (zeta 0.40-0.77, audit_c2 a).
6. **The two reports on Cummins agree once stated precisely.**
   - The memory kernel K adds without double counting.
   - The infinite-frequency added mass must come from one theory (Kramers-Kronig).
   - At rest the 2D+t infinite-frequency added mass (7877 kg) is about 1.9x the 3D value (about 3.9-4.1 t). So K from 3D BEM cannot simply be added to v0's m_a: the 3D added-mass factor J of section 2.6 is required.
7. **Ventilation band.** The literature report used the static transom draught (Fn_T 3/4/5 at 11.3/15.1/18.9 kn); the audit used the running draught (Fn_T 3.12 at 13 kn). The design uses the instantaneous draught, giving a band of about 11-18 kn.

## 2. Target formulation, term by term

### 2.0 Notation and states
- State layout as v0: 14 states `[x,y,z,roll,pitch,yaw,u,v,w,p,q,r,thrust,nozzle]`.
- Hidden states:
  - radiation memory states x_r (about 60-100);
  - the mean-speed filter U_bar (G5.4);
  - pump prime (v0).
- eps is not a state: it is quasi-steady.
- Stations, SP and Th as pv:91-111.
- u_r = u - u_orb(x, z_c) is the relative surge inflow; v_r = v + x r - v_orb(x, z_c) the relative lateral velocity. Orbital velocities are linear-wave values at the section's effective depth z_c (centroid of the immersed section). This is the same depth used for the wave pressure, so there is no double Smith decay (A7).

### 2.1 Kinematics and relative velocity (replaces pv:262-264, 313-314)
- d = eta(x,t) - (Z_rest + z + z_k + SP x Th) — unchanged.
- tau = k' + SP Th — unchanged.
- V = w_orb(x, z_c) - (w + SP x q) + u_r tau.
- In long waves w_orb → eta_t, so v0's V is the long-wave limit.
- Basis: relative-motion 2D+t (Zarnick 1978; Faltinsen 2005 ch. 9). Small trim and roll as v0.
- Matters most at low speed and in surf-riding, where u_orb/u is not small (about ±0.6 m/s against 2 m/s at 4 kn in Hs 1 m).

### 2.2 Hydrostatics and Froude-Krylov force: exact pressure integration (replaces buoy pv:279 in Xp, Xw pv:300-302, Ysec pv:301-304)
- Pressure p = rho g (-z) + p_I(x,y,z,t), with p_I the linear incident pressure including its depth decay e^{kz} and its variation across the beam.
- F = -∫_{S_w} p n dS over the instantaneous wetted surface: the bottom and sides of every station (closed-form contour integrals for the V-plus-vertical-side polygon, heeled) PLUS the wetted part of the transom face.
- Moments taken about the CG at the actual pressure centroids.
- Forces normal to the keel keep moment x·f exactly, as in v0. The face force adds -z_cT F_T.
- Longitudinal identity (calm water and long-wave limit, proved by applying the divergence theorem to each slice):

    X_bottom = -rho g a_b [ Q_T + ∫ A ∂eta/∂x dx ],   X_face = + rho g Q_T,wet(eps)

  Here Q_T is the first moment of the wetted transom area about the local surface. It is computed in the telescoped form, with no geometric derivative.
  - Wet transom (eps = 1, a_b = 1): X = -rho g ∫ A eta_x dx, the textbook closed-body FK force, and exactly 0 at rest.
  - Dry transom (eps = 0): X = -a_b rho g (Q_T + ∫A eta_x dx), which is v0's buoyancy-tilt Xp, i.e. Savitsky's hydrostatic part of Delta tan tau.
  - Xw is deleted at all speeds.
- Vertical force and pitch: the same integral. The heave/pitch hydrostatics reproduce v0 (stiffness 87862 vs BEM 87738 N/m) and BEM C55 to 0.2 %.
- Lateral force, roll and yaw: the same integral over the heeled sections. This replaces the centreline-slope Ysec and the "roll about the wave slope" term. Lateral FK is no longer multiplied by a_b, which is a vertical-planing concept with no basis laterally.
- Validity: linear incident waves, instantaneous wetted geometry (weakly nonlinear FK, as `sim/sections.py`). Exact in the linear limit against Capytaine's FK, which gives the check.

### 2.3 Transom flow state eps (empirical closure 1; new)
- eps = h_w/d_T ∈ [0,1]: wetted height over transom immersion.
- eps = E(Fn_T), with Fn_T = u_r,T / sqrt(g d_T) on the INSTANTANEOUS transom immersion.
- E is digitised from Doctors et al. 2007 (ISP 54:145-163), anchored by Maki et al. 2005 (ventilation at Fn_T 3-3.5) and Saunders 1957 (4-5).
- Pressure in the dead-water region is hydrostatic about its own lowered free surface. This gives Q_T,wet, which falls continuously to 0 at full ventilation.
- eps_bar = E evaluated on the calm steady branch at the mean speed U_bar. It indexes the global radiation regime (2.6); the instantaneous eps indexes local pressures (2.2, 2.4).
- Basis: measured flow-topology change. It is viscous and separated, so CFD or experiment is the only first-principles route.
- Validity: transom-stern models at model-scale Reynolds number. Scale effect is an open question.
- Hysteresis (Maki) is added only if the source supports it. Quasi-steady with no lag, so no new time constant.

### 2.4 Hydrostatic realisation a_b (empirical closure 2 plus bridging assumption A; replaces pv:246-248)
- a_b = a_dry + (1 - a_dry) eps, with a_dry = 0.5 (Zarnick 1978; validated in this repo on Fridsma cases A and B, DEFECTS H7).
- Exact at both ends:
  - a_b = 1 is closed-body hydrostatics (a wet transom carries its face pressure);
  - 0.5 is the documented dry-transom planing value.
- The linear form in between is an assumption, not a derivation. It replaces an interval in C_V that had no physical basis with a measured physical state.
- Supporting physics: Savitsky's buoyant-lift term 0.0055 λ^2.5 τ^1.1 / C_V^2 corresponds to a hydrostatic realisation of about 0.45-0.7 for λ of 1.5-4 at 4 deg. Used only to explain, not inside the model.
- First-principles replacement if S8 shows this assumption carries the result: an offline per-station 2D+t computation with gravity (Sun & Faltinsen 2007), or Sun & Faltinsen's 0.5 B transom correction.

### 2.5 2D+t impulsive and momentum force with a complete D/Dt (replaces pv:172-173, 277, 280)
- f_dyn = D/Dt(m_a V)^+ + 1/2 rho C_dc 2c V|V|.
- D/Dt(m_a V) = m_a DV/Dt + V·Dm_a/Dt.
- Dm_a/Dt = (∂m_a/∂d) V - u_r (∂m_a/∂x)|_d.
- The momentum-flux part V·Dm_a/Dt counts only on entry: while V > 0 (Zarnick's entry-only rule, pv:277) AND Dm_a/Dt > 0. With V > 0 and u ≥ 0 both parts of Dm_a/Dt are ≥ 0, so on entry the flux is never negative. It is identical to pv:277 wherever ∂m_a/∂x|_d = 0.
  - *Decision (S1 review, 2026-10-06).* The first implementation gated on Dm_a/Dt > 0 alone. At a chine-wetted taper station Dm_a/Dt = u|∂m_a/∂x| > 0 for any V, so a section rising out (V < 0) carried a suction V·u|∂m_a/∂x| < 0 that grows without limit with bow-down trim. v0 applies no exit flux anywhere. That suction created new sunk bow-down equilibria at 12-35 kn, nose-dives from bow-down starts at 16-30 kn, and divergence in following seas. The exit rule is therefore entry only. A ventilation-bounded suction would be a new, registered assumption; none is adopted.
- *Discretisation of the new term (S1 review).* v0 decides chine wetting at each station's centre. With the complete D/Dt, a taper strip's flux would jump, as its centre caps, from ∂m_a/∂d·V² to u·V·|∂m_a/∂x| (several times larger), and the boat would settle on that threshold instead of an equilibrium (sliding states; station-count-dependent attitude at 7.5-9.5 and 28-40 kn). So, over each strip of the forebody taper:
  - d is taken linear (centre value and slope) and the chine analytic, and the chine-wetting point(s) are located exactly (an ellipse cut by a line).
  - Chine-wetted part: u·V·k_a·π/2·ρ·[yc²(aft end) − yc²(fwd end)]/dx. It sums exactly along the taper.
  - Other part: V²·∫∂m_a/∂d dx / dx.
  - Both parts are continuous in the state.
  - A strip with no chine-wetted point keeps v0's centre value bit for bit. So does every prismatic strip: their own centre-decided cap jump is v0's, and fixing it is a separate decision (Q5) because it moves the 14-25 kn equilibria.
- DV/Dt as pv:280, with u replaced by u_r and the orbital accelerations included.
- The added mass stays on the left of the equations (pv:338-353), recomputed every step.
- Integration by parts: the new term equals the transom momentum flux u m_aT V_T. Linearised pitch damping D55 at 4 kn goes from -6220 to +16745 N m s, against U x_T^2 m_aT = 16166 (audit_c2 b).
- m_a(x,d) = J_epsbar(x)·k_a π/2 rho c^2, with J_epsbar = 1 + eps_bar (J(x) - 1) (section 2.6).
- k_a = 1.0 and C_dc = cos beta are Zarnick's, unchanged.
- The Wagner cap is unchanged in as1, and so is the centre-decided chine-wetting rule at prismatic strips (deferred, section 7, Q5). Only the taper strips locate chine wetting inside the strip, as above, because the new term lives there.

### 2.6 Low-speed gravity radiation and 3D correction (bridging assumption B; new)
- J(x): ratio of the sectional 3D infinite-frequency added mass (from the ω=∞ solution, sectional pressure integration) to the plant's own m_a at the rest attitude. Both the total and the distribution must match, so A33, A35 and A55 at rest all agree with 3D. Expected J ≈ 0.5 on average (7877 kg vs about 3.9-4.1 t).
- F_mem = -eps_bar ∫ K_3D(t-s) [nu(s) - nu_bar(s)] ds, as a state-space model:
  - surge input measured from the mean speed (G5.4, `sim/vessel.py:754-765`);
  - exact matrix-exponential step (G5.6, `sim/vessel.py:775-801`).
- Delta F_D = eps_bar Σ_j a_j Re{[F_D,3D(ω_j, β_j) - F_D,plant(ω_j, β_j)] e^{i φ_j(t)}}: the per-component diffraction difference against the plant's own linear diffraction at infinite frequency, at the relative heading (`sim/wavefield.py:118`, F4).
- Basis: Cummins with infinite-frequency added mass from one theory (Kramers-Kronig), and the time-dependent added mass layered on a linear method (de Jong 2011 §6.3).
- By construction the zero-speed linearisation equals the 3D BEM, which gives an exact gate.
- Validity: Fn_L ≤ 0.3 for zero-speed coefficients (Fossen 2005). eps_bar keeps them on to Fn_L about 0.55-0.7, beyond that stated limit; flagged and tested in S8.
- At eps_bar = 0 all three corrections vanish identically.
- Principled replacements, deferred: speed-scheduled gravity memory from linear 2.5D theory (Faltinsen & Zhao 1991; check against Troesch 1992), or a 3D forward-speed transient Green function (PANSHIP-type).

### 2.7 Steady gravity correction: wave resistance, sinkage and trim through the hump (new)
- Delta F_s(U, z, θ) = [thin-ship with gravity] - [thin-ship phi=0 limit], for R_w, F_z and M_y.
- Thin-ship means Michell over the as1 offsets (numerical, generalising `hydro/kelvin.py`), a Havelock pressure-distribution term for the dynamic lift (cross term included), and Doctors' transom-hollow extension for eps < 1.
- Tabulated offline; applied quasi-steadily at the instantaneous attitude and the mean speed.
- At Fn → 0 it gives double-body suction (sinkage with the right sign); at Fn ~ 0.4-0.6 it gives the hump; it vanishes at high Fn.
- No double count with the phi=0 2D+t (the "Kelvin note"), because the phi=0 part is subtracted.
- Limit: thin-ship at B/L 0.375 is doubtful. Acceptance comes from series data (S6). The upgrade path is a 3D steady panel method or CFD tabulation (user decision).

### 2.8 Viscous terms (empirical, documented)
- Friction: ITTC-57 + 0.0004 on the wetted girth (pv:285-296), with u_r.
- Lateral cross-flow drag: C_D 1.0 on v_r (Hooft-type; jet boats spin at zero speed, H2).
- Air drag as v0.
- Roll chine-eddy damping: Ikeda & Katayama 2000 hard-chine eddy component with range check, or a decay test. Its lift component is NOT used, because the asymmetric 2D+t of 2.9 produces it.
- Astern: the transom face leads, with hydrostatic face plus bluff-face drag (Hoerner, Fluid-Dynamic Drag).

### 2.9 Lateral plane and roll (replaces pv:313-325, 371-383, gz:221-238)
- **Lateral added masses** A22, A26, A66, A24, A44 are instantaneous, from 2D phi=0 section coefficients at the actual heeled immersion, times J_lat,epsbar (3D at rest, from BEM). They sit on the left of the equations.
- **Lateral sectional force:** -D/Dt(m22 v_r), the Munk form, giving lift at the trailing edge (transom for u > 0, stem for u < 0, where m22 → 0 gives no lift but keeps the Munk moment). The orbital part of v_r gives lateral diffraction and the wave yaw moment, the broaching moment.
- **Coriolis:** added-mass Coriolis in the yaw-rotating frame only (E11): (m + A22) v r + A26 r^2 in surge, -(m + A11) u r in sway.
- **Two-way roll:**
  - lateral forces act at their centre-of-pressure heights, which gives heel in turns and beam seas;
  - heeled-section hydrostatics from per-station polygon tables (`_immersed`, gz), exact; this reduces to the v0 GZ table at the rest trim;
  - an asymmetric 2D+t per side, with wetted half-beams c_L and c_R at heel (Toyama 1993; Xu, Troesch & Vorus 1998; validated by Marleaux et al. 2024 against Brown & Klosinski 1994), gives dynamic roll restoring and lift damping at speed, and feeds heel back into heave and pitch.
- **Roll wave damping:** the 3D memory (eps_bar).
- **Validity:** small drift; heel as far as the section tables go; asymmetric Wagner checked only at FnB 3-4 by data.

### 2.10 Surge inertia and astern
- A11 = eps_bar A11,3D + (1 - eps_bar)·0.05 m. The second value is v0's labelled placeholder; its effect on planing dynamics is under 4 % of surge inertia.
- Astern is handled per 2.5, 2.8 and 2.9; the D/Dt rule is valid for either sign of u_r.

### 2.11 Waterjet
- v0 momentum model (wj:92-106) unchanged above a cavitation-limited thrust envelope.
- The envelope comes from documented chart data (the dashed cavitation boundary of the HamiltonJet 291 chart, `studies/exp_waterjet.py:43`) or the pump's NPSH data. It lives in a new jet module.
- Thrust deduction and intake lift remain listed as missing.

### 2.12 Steady states
- Newton on the captive equations, using the free-DOF mass block (G5.1), plus pseudo-arclength continuation in U. This gives branches, folds and eigenvalues.
- running_attitude, initial_state and calm_resistance use the stable branch reached by slow acceleration from rest. A `branch=` option is added.
- v0's 4000-step captive run (pv:409-421) and its "first guess" captive force (pv:343-346) are replaced.

### 2.13 Numerics and cost
- Heun at dt 0.02 s and lateral substeps as v0. The substep test uses instantaneous lateral inertia, which should make substeps rare (defect K).
- Memory states are stepped exactly.
- Budget: at most 2x v0, i.e. ≤ 4 ms per plant step in the task sea (v0 1.95 ms) and at least 5x real time.

- **minor** — Some statements are stronger than the physics supports.

- **minor** — Smaller consistency gaps.

## Review (validation lens)

**Verdict.** The physics core of the design is careful. The validation plan, however, cannot yet support the claim of a plant that is physically credible at any speed for scarab195.

What holds up:
- I re-ran the v0 regression results myself. golden_v0, the hand and m0 rows on s0_l0 and s0_l1, the onestep stream and turn_gz are all bit-identical, and the lofi fingerprint 36.50273627118908 matches exactly.
- The four frozen sha1 values belong to sim/planing_vessel.py, sim/planing_vessel_gz.py, sim/waterjet.py and sim/actuators.py.

Main problems:
1. **Below about 15 kn there is no experimental data that covers this hull.**
   - Radojčić 2014 is open access and I read it. Its appendix-2 boundary 10 excludes the plant's combination of L/B 2.67 and LCG/L 0.404.
   - That paper also abandoned its trim model because the TUNS and USCG trims "disagree to a great extent".
   - The placeholder bow is elliptic with no stem rise. At rest the waterline half-angle is 38-57 deg over the last 0.4 m.
2. **The main independent planing case falls inside the new ventilation band.** Fridsma case A has Fn_T 4.24 at keel depth (4.84 at mean depth), and so do Fridsma 1971 conditions 21-23. Those cases therefore test the E(Fn_T) closure, not "as1 equals v0".
3. **Several acceptance criteria are tuned to v0, one-sided, or fail by construction.**
   - P1 asks for R within ±10 %, but v0 is -15 % at 20 kn.
   - P2 allows ±6 % while v0 sits at +5.7 %. P2 is also not independent: hydro/hulls.py:139-141 chose the hull unknowns so that Savitsky plus the jet reproduce the measured top speed.
   - P7 only requires "not below v0".
   - The energy test fails at dt 0.02 because of the Heun integrator alone.
   - Several rest-state gates pass by construction.
4. **The S8 bridge test has no failing outcome, and its result is already known.** Its accept/flag rule cannot fail, and design_check part 3 already shows a_b moves trim by about 2.4 deg at 10 kn. Choosing among the variants by fit to data would be tuning.
5. **The ventilation and jet-cavitation sources do not match this hull or pump.**
   - The design evaluates Fn_T on the instantaneous running draught, at a depth it does not define. Doctors 2007 used five simplified transom models, and secondary users define Fn_T on the static draught.
   - The cited cavitation boundary of the HamiltonJet 291 chart was never digitised in the repo, and it belongs to a different, larger pump.
6. **The "fold removed" claim was checked with the v0 a_b blend and no face force.** Even then, unstable bow-down sunk roots remain at 12-25 kn.
7. **Versioning has three gaps.**
   - There are two plant selectors: the USV_PHYSICS environment variable and the hull key. The frozen meta caches are keyed only on code, and sim/lofi.py overwrites the cache file named after the hull.
   - There is no golden for as1 itself.
   - The goldens are gitignored and the Python environment is not pinned.
